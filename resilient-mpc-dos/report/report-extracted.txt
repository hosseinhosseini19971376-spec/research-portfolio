                              C ONCLUSION
   As can be seen from the results obtained from numerical
examples, the proposed resilient algorithm is responsive to
different systems in multi-agent modes and greatly reduces
the effects of the DoS attack. This increase speed to respond
to the system shows itself when using of laguerre controller.
The effect of this controller on different Dos attacks was
investigated for different examples. For future projects, this
resilience algorithm can be implemented on other systems or
experimental tests can be performed on it. The algorithm can
also be used to reduce the effects of other attacks, such as fals
data injrption attack.
